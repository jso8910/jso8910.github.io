The true level of play a player or team has. So a team might have a *true talent* of being a .500 team, but due to [[./Random Variation|Random Variation]] it may actually win 84 games. This also applies to players (eg being a .300 hitter).

As discussed by [[./TangoTiger|TangoTiger]] in [[./sources/Run Values by Number of Prior Pitches Thrown|Run Values By Number of Prior Pitches Thrown]], true talent can change from game to game! So in reality, when you (attempt to) measure true talent, you're actually measuring the average true talent over a large sample.
