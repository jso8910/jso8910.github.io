**Win Estimation** is the pursuit of attempting to estimate the number of games a team will win. This can either be on a per-game basis (e.g. [[wiki/Win Expectancy|win expectancy]]) or on a per-season basis (e.g. [[wiki/Projections|projections]], [[wiki/WAR|WAR]]).
