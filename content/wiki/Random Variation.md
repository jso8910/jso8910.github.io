The factors outside a player or team's control which cause a deviation between [[./True Talent|True Talent]] and actual results. Some factors in random variation include [[91 Baseball/Fielding|the opposing fielders]] ([[91 Baseball/BABIP|BABIP]] luck), [[91 Baseball/Weather|weather factors]], and, for pitchers and teams, [[91 Baseball/Sequencing Luck|sequencing luck]].
