The factors outside a player or team's control which cause a deviation between [[/content/wiki/True Talent|True Talent]] and actual results. Some factors in random variation include [[wiki/Fielding|the opposing fielders]] ([[wiki/BABIP|BABIP]] luck), [[wiki/Weather|weather factors]], and, for pitchers and teams, [[wiki/Sequencing Luck|sequencing luck]].
