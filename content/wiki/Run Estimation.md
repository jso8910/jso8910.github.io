[[/content/wiki/Win Estimation|Win Estimation]] is hard, especially for individual players. Really hard. So instead, we can use **run estimation**. For players, the most common strategy is to estimate the number of runs they were worth and then plug that into a formula for [[wiki/Runs Per Win|Runs Per Win]] and then boom, you get a win estimation. For teams, you can also do that. But there are also a broader set of strategies available. You can use [[wiki/Projections|projections]] (commonly [[wiki/Simulation|simulation]]) in order to reflect the realities of a team's schedule, you can use [[/content/wiki/Pythagorean Record|Pythagorean Record]] if you want a simple estimation of [[/content/wiki/True Talent|True Talent]] wins (ie wins against a .500 team), but it may not be as reflective of injuries, strength of schedule, etc.
